Trust Layer
Event detail — Stellar-verified
Chain-Confirmed Escrow
The TicketContract locks funds at purchase. No release happens until conditions are met — or a verified refund is issued. Supabase mirrors state; it never authorizes the action.
Atomic Resale Settlement
The MarketplaceContract settles price, royalty, and ownership transfer in a single atomic operation. There is no window for a partial or dishonest outcome.
Three-Gate QR Validation
Local payload/signature check → on-chain owner & status → organizer-signed mark_used(). All three must pass before the door opens.

Every Event
Needs Trusted Ticketing
A structural trust gap exists across every event category — concerts, conferences, sports. No market segment is immune.
Attendees need confidence
Every ticket buyer deserves proof of ownership, a recovery path if payment fails, and a fair refund when an event is cancelled. These are structural expectations — not premium features.
Organizers & Venues need control
Organizers need auditable sales, enforceable resale rules, and reliable payout. Venues need entry that cannot be disputed — a QR that proves the original, unreplayed ticket.
The thesis
PulseGate addresses a lifecycle trust gap that exists wherever tickets are sold. This is a sourced structural thesis — no unverified market-size figures.
