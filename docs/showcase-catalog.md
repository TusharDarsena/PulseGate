# PulseGate Showcase Catalog

This catalog is fictional judge-facing demonstration content published on
Stellar Testnet. It does not represent real events, venues, attendance, or
commercial partnerships.

All events use:

- Timezone: `Asia/Kolkata`
- Network: `StellarTestnet`
- Refund policy: `cancelled_event_original_price`
- Resale policy: `stellar_marketplace_unlocked`
- Support contact: `support@pulsegate.test`
- Accessibility: Step-free entry and an accessible check-in lane are available.
- Entry: Bring the rotating PulseGate ticket QR and a matching photo ID. Doors
  close 30 minutes after the published start time.
- Prohibited items: Outside alcohol, weapons, professional recording equipment,
  and large bags.
- Public links and map URL: empty

`https://stellar-gamma-weld.vercel.app/demo-media/events/`.
